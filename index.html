DRISHTI

An agentic vision-language system for natural-language interpretation of satellite imagery, built for the ISRO GeoNLI problem statement — chat-based analysis across Optical, Infrared, and SAR data.

Technical Highlights
  • DRISHTI-GCV — a custom difficulty-aware dataset of ~180K samples spanning grounding, captioning, and VQA.
  • Two-stage curriculum: LoRA/QLoRA-tuned Qwen3-VL backbone + DPO alignment for hallucination reduction.
  • Captioning BERT-BLEU 0.8493 — beats GPT-4o (0.8174); VQA accuracy +21% over GeoChat.
  • SAM3 grounding module with pyramidal tiling for object counting and area estimation (~89% at low density).
  • vLLM serving with KV caching and concurrent batching: 2-3x lower latency at ~95% GPU utilization.
  • Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0 — IIT Kharagpur overall champions; team placed 4th on the ISRO GeoNLI PS.

TRACE.ai

Autonomous anti-money-laundering at the speed of transactions — detects illicit fund flows on temporal graphs, explains every alert with evidence, and auto-generates FIU-IND compliant STR reports.

What it does
  • Graph pattern matcher for five AML typologies: circular flow, layering chains, structuring clusters, mule fan-in/out, dormant burst.

  • Auto-STR generation: 4-6 hours of manual tracing reduced to about 5 minutes.
  • NL investigation copilot (Gemini) translating plain-English questions into graph queries; SHAP-style explanations on every alert.
  • Hot-reloadable YAML compliance engine for CTR/KYC/RBI rules.

Smart Meter Data Analytics

Research at MicroDERLab (Politehnica University of Bucharest, via IIT Kharagpur FTP 9.0) on anomaly detection and non-intrusive load monitoring over 1-second residential smart-meter data.

Highlights
  • 15-detector dual-validation benchmark (Matrix Profile, IsolationForest, LOF, OCSVM, ECOD, COPOD, HBOS, AutoEncoder + CWT features) over 20.9M+ samples.
  • Key finding: synthetic injection benchmarks overstate unsupervised AD — on real human-verified labels, only a variance filter (0.129) and ECOD (0.161) beat the base rate.
  • CWT time-frequency features add genuine orthogonal value on shape and dropout anomalies that learned detectors miss.
  • Trained a Seq2Point CNN for load disaggregation on a real synchronous sub-meter pair — an honest negative result that motivates appliance-level sub-metering.
  • Under Prof. Grigore Stamatescu; builds on the CWT pipeline from CPRNet.

AgentGuard

Guardian middleware that sits between AI agents and their actions, intercepting and validating every operation before execution. 2nd Runner Up at Microsoft AI Unlocked 2026.

4-Layer Defense
  • L1 Input: prompt-injection detection (33-regex pre-filter + Azure Prompt Shields), harmful-content filters, blocklists.
  • L2 Output: PII detection, toxicity filtering, LLM-as-judge hallucination and groundedness checks.
  • L3 Tool Firewall: entity recognition on tool args, contrastive indirect-PI detection (MELON), HITL/AITL approval gate.
  • L4 RBAC + Behavioral: ABAC engine (ALLOW/DENY/ELEVATE) and a 5-signal anomaly detector.
  • Covers the OWASP Agentic AI Top 10 with a live OpenTelemetry trace stream and audit log.
